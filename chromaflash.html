1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ChromaFlash collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the ChromaFlash Android application.

ChromaFlash is a screen-and-LED color flashlight, strobe, SOS beacon, and party light application. We designed the app to follow data minimization principles: - You can use ChromaFlash's core free features (Screen light, Camera LED Flash, Red/Yellow/Green presets, 0–25 Hz strobe dial, SOS signal, brightness control, and Eco/Battery Saver) without signing in or providing personal information. - Personal account and purchase data are collected only if you choose to sign in with Google to purchase paid upgrades (individual colors, Color Palette, or Party Mode) and sync those unlocks across your devices.

2. Information We Collect & Why

A. Free / Signed-Out Usage (Guest Mode)

When you use ChromaFlash without signing in: - No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted. - No account identifiers, cloud database calls, or billing requests are made. - Flashlight settings (such as brightness, selected free color, strobe frequency, screen/flash output selection, and dark/eco toggles) stay in active device memory during your session.

B. Account & Authentication Data (When You Sign In with Google)

To buy paid features and restore or sync them across multiple phones or tablets, ChromaFlash uses Google Sign-In (via Android Credential Manager) and Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we process: - Firebase User ID (uid): A unique account identifier generated by Firebase Authentication to link your unlocked features to your account. - Email Address: Your Google account email address, used to identify your account and displayed inside the Store and Account dialogs. - Display Name: Your Google profile name (if available), displayed in the Account dialog. - Authentication Tokens: Short-lived Google ID tokens and Firebase ID tokens used solely to verify your identity during sign-in, Cloud Firestore access, and purchase verification.

C. In-App Purchase & Entitlement Data

ChromaFlash offers permanent in-app unlocks (individual preset colors, full Color Palette, and Party Mode) processed exclusively through Google Play Billing.

When you complete an in-app purchase while signed in, we verify the transaction and store the minimum entitlement and anti-fraud records required in Firebase Cloud Firestore (and, when enabled, via our Firebase Cloud Function verifyPlayPurchase): - Entitlement State (/users/{userId}/entitlements/state): - Your Firebase userId - List of unlocked color IDs (unlockedColorIds) - Whether Color Palette (isColorPaletteUnlocked) and Party Mode (isPartyModeUnlocked) are unlocked - SHA-256 hash of your most recent purchase token (lastPurchaseId) and server timestamps (createdAt, updatedAt) - Verified Purchase Receipt Record (/users/{userId}/verified_purchases/{purchaseId}): - Your Firebase userId - Purchased Google Play productId (e.g., color_cyan, color_palette, party_mode) - Google Play orderId, purchaseToken, packageName, priceInr, currency (INR), purchaseTimeMillis, verificationStatus, verificationMethod, and createdAt timestamp - Anti-Replay Token Lock (/purchase_tokens/{purchaseId}): - Cryptographic SHA-256 hash (purchaseId) of the Google Play purchaseToken, paired with userId, productId, orderId, and createdAt - Used strictly to prevent the same Google Play purchase token from being claimed or replayed across multiple accounts.

Payment Details We Do NOT Collect:
All financial transactions are handled directly by Google Play. ChromaFlash never receives, collects, views, or stores your credit/debit card numbers, bank details, UPI IDs, or billing address.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

ChromaFlash requests only the permissions strictly required for its lighting and cloud-sync features:

Permission Purpose Data Collected?
CAMERA (android.permission.CAMERA) & FLASHLIGHT Used exclusively via Android CameraManager.setTorchMode() to toggle your device's rear LED flash ON/OFF when Flash output is selected. No. ChromaFlash does not take photos, record video, preview camera feeds, or access/store any camera frames.
INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET) Used exclusively for Google Sign-In, syncing your unlocked features with Firebase Cloud Firestore, fetching live store pricing/catalog items, and verifying Google Play purchases. Only account, entitlement, and purchase verification data described in Section 2.

Display & Window Behavior:
While the flashlight is turned ON, ChromaFlash temporarily keeps the screen awake (FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON) and adjusts screen brightness (screenBrightness). When you turn off the light, minimize the app, or close it, the light and brightness overrides automatically turn off.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

ChromaFlash does not: - Collect photos, videos, audio, or microphone recordings - Collect precise or approximate location (GPS, Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth beacons) - Access contacts, SMS, call logs, files, or media libraries - Integrate third-party advertising SDKs or collect your Android Advertising ID (AAID) - Track you across third-party apps or websites - Sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We share data only with essential infrastructure providers required to operate authentication, cloud sync, and billing: - Google LLC: - Google Play Billing & Google Play Developer API: Processes payments, provides in-app product pricing, and verifies purchase validity. - Google Sign-In / Android Credential Manager & Firebase Authentication: Authenticates your account. - Google Cloud Firestore & Firebase Cloud Functions: Stores your synced entitlements, verified purchase records, and anti-replay purchase locks.

Data may also be disclosed only if required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect against fraud and unauthorized access.

6. Data Security

We implement technical and architectural safeguards to protect your data: - Encrypted Transit: All network communication with Google Sign-In, Google Play, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and verification endpoints uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. - Strict Access Controls: Firestore Security Rules restrict read and write access to your personal entitlement and purchase documents exclusively to your authenticated Firebase user ID (request.auth.uid == userId). - Purchase Integrity: In-app purchase receipts are validated against package identity, product pricing, cryptographic Play signatures / Play Developer API verification, and SHA-256 anti-replay token locks.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Retention: Account authentication references, unlocked feature entitlements, and verified purchase records are retained while your account remains active so you can continue using purchased features across devices.
  • Signing Out: You can sign out at any time from the Account dialog in the app (Sign Out / Switch Account), which signs you out of Firebase Authentication and clears your local Credential Manager session.
  • Requesting Data & Account Deletion:
    You can request deletion of your ChromaFlash account and associated Firestore entitlement records by emailing smallsimple.app@gmail.com from (or identifying) the Google email address linked to your account.
  • Upon verification, we will delete your account and personal entitlement profile (/users/{uid}/entitlements/state).
  • Please note that anonymized/hashed transaction records or order IDs may be retained where strictly necessary to prevent purchase replay fraud, resolve billing disputes, or comply with legal, tax, and Google Play accounting obligations.
  • Note that uninstalling the app removes local app data from your device, while cloud-synced purchases remain linked to your Google account unless you request deletion (and purchases can always be restored via Google Play when signed into the same account).

8. Children's Privacy

ChromaFlash is a general-utility flashlight app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has signed in or provided personal information, please contact smallsimple.app@gmail.com so we can promptly delete the account data.

9. International Data Transfers

Your authentication and cloud-synced entitlement data are processed on Google Cloud / Firebase servers, which may operate in regions outside your country of residence, subject to Google's data protection frameworks and safeguards.
